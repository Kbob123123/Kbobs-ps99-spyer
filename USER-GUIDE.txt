PS99 DISCORD BOT — USER GUIDE
================================
This is for regular members of a server that has the bot — not admins.
If you want to change what channels the bot posts in, that's an admin
task, see SETUP-SERVER.txt (you'll need "Manage Server" permission).

All commands are slash commands — type "/" in any channel and pick from
the list, or type the full command name.


COMMANDS YOU CAN USE
------------------------

/rap item:<name>
    Look up the Recent Average Price (RAP) for an item or pet. Partial
    names work — e.g. "/rap item:huge cosmic" will match anything with
    "huge cosmic" in the name.
    Example: /rap item:Unicorn Dragon

/pet name:<name>
    Look up ONE specific pet — shows exists count and RAP for each of its
    variants (Normal/Golden/Rainbow/Shiny), its tier (Huge/Titanic/
    Gargantuan) if it has one, its thumbnail, and two charts: exists over
    time and RAP over time. An exact name match is used if there is one;
    otherwise the closest partial match. Charts fill in gradually the more
    this command or the tracker checks that pet — expect "not enough
    history yet" on the first lookup.
    Example: /pet name:turtle

/clan name:<exact clan name>
    Stats for a specific clan — members, points, deposited diamonds,
    country, plus the clan's icon. Needs the exact clan name (not partial).
    Example: /clan name:EXP

/clantop10
    Shows the top 10 clans by points, each with an EXACT hourly point
    gain (not an estimate — see "How rates are calculated" below), member
    count, and how long until they'd overtake the clan ranked just above
    them. Includes a chart of the top 5 clans' points over time. Rates
    won't be available until the bot has been running for at least an
    hour — it'll say "gathering data..." until then.

/league name:<league name>
    Look up ONE specific league by name (exact match preferred, partial
    match falls back). Shows points, level, members, contributor count,
    an exact hourly point rate for the league, and a chart of its points
    over time. Note: the public API only exposes league-level stats —
    there's no per-player breakdown available, so this shows the league
    as a whole, not individual member contributions.
    Example: /league name:Snaks

/leaguetop10
    Same idea as /clantop10 but for the top 10 leagues by points.

/battle
    Shows the currently active clan battle and its placement rewards.
    Note: this data can occasionally lag a few hours behind the actual
    in-game battle — that's a known quirk of the underlying API, not a
    bot bug.

HOW RATES ARE CALCULATED
-----------------------------
Every "hourly rate" shown by this bot (pet hatch rates, clan/league point
rates) is an EXACT count over the trailing 60 minutes, recalculated every
10 minutes — not an estimate. For example, a check at 7:10 covers exactly
6:10 through 7:10; the next check at 7:20 covers 6:20 through 7:20, and so
on. Because of this, rates are only shown once a full hour of history has
built up — expect up to an hour of "gathering data..." after the bot
(re)starts, which is intentional so nothing misleading gets reported in
that window.


AUTOMATIC CHANNELS (you don't need to do anything for these)
------------------------------------------------------------------
If your server has set them up, you may see the bot posting on its own
in certain channels:

    - Huge / Titanic / Gargantuan rate channels: recalculates every 10
      minutes and shows the EXACT number of each pet hatched in the last
      60 minutes (not an estimate) — e.g. a post at 7:10 covers 6:10-7:10,
      the next at 7:20 covers 6:20-7:20, and so on. Takes up to an hour
      after the bot starts before the first post shows up, since it needs
      a full hour of history first.
    - RAP alert channel: posts only when a Huge/Titanic/Gargantuan pet's
      RAP swings by 200% or more (a big price move).
    - Exists alert channel: posts only when a Huge/Titanic/Gargantuan
      pet's exists count more than doubles within 10 minutes (usually
      means a new one just got added to the game, or a rare mass-hatch
      event).
    - Clan Top 10 channel: posts the current top 10 clans every 10
      minutes, same info as running /clantop10 yourself.
    - League Top 10 channel: posts the current top 10 league standings
      every 10 minutes, same info as running /leaguetop10 yourself.

These are automatic — you can't trigger them manually, and it's normal
for the alert channels to stay quiet for long stretches since they only
fire on big changes. Most of these posts include a chart image and, where
relevant, a thumbnail (top pet/clan icon) — if an image fails to load,
the text portion of the post is still accurate; it just means the
chart-rendering or Roblox-thumbnail service was temporarily unreachable.


TIPS
-------
- /rap uses broad matching and can show several results at once (good for
  comparing similar items). /pet and /league resolve to ONE specific
  match — an exact name if it exists, otherwise the closest partial match.
- /clan needs an exact name since there's no fuzzy search on clans.
- If a command says it "couldn't fetch" something, the underlying PS99 API
  might be temporarily down or rate-limited — try again in a minute.
